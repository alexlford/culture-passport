# Contribution rules

- Create a feature branch and pull request for every change. Do not push directly to `main`.
- Before merging, verify that `validate` and `Browser smoke · visitor preview` both completed successfully on the exact current PR head commit. Missing, pending, skipped, cancelled or failing checks are not passing checks.
- Refresh PR state before merging, supply its expected head SHA, and preserve concurrent contributions. Verify checks on the merged `main` commit before reporting it green.
- Fix the cause of failed checks; do not disable checks or weaken their guarantees to merge.
- Keep the canonical source repository private. Preserve the visitor-only export and explicit story-review approval gate. A passing build does not authorize publication or a visibility change.
- Preserve confirmed, uncertain, negative and unanswered interview states distinctly; reservation evidence is not proof of attendance or an exact visit date.
- Keep Culture, Work, Adventures and Life Map independently maintained. Link related experiences instead of duplicating their canonical datasets.

These are contributor instructions, not GitHub-enforced branch protection.

## Mandatory interview pre-filter (persistent)

- Before asking about any previously unanswered restaurant, brewery, pub, cocktail bar, café or other venue, verify eligibility against the established Culture Passport recognition criteria **first**. Only present award-qualified, previously unanswered and deduplicated venues, unless the user expressly asks for an exception. Do not pad five-item rounds with unqualified places.
- Preserve official evidence for the precise award, year, category, recipient, ranking and URL. A chef award is not a restaurant award, a beer medal is not a taproom award, and an editorial directory listing is not an independently judged award. Michelin Selected alone is not interchangeable with a Star or Bib Gourmand.
- Screen against the private visit, maybe, nonattendance, wishlist and interview ledgers before proposing candidates. Interview with relevant real photos, short recognition-grounded descriptions, all four attendance options and a working five-response Submit control.
- The user's 'Wishlist' response is explicit nonattendance plus future intent; keep it private. Confirmed visits, even without qualifying awards, must be added to public visitor candidate records for review, without bypassing the separate story-review publication gate. Keep dates unknown unless supported.
